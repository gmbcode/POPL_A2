## 4. Render an expression tree

```text
Apply skills/p2-arithmetic-equations/SKILL.md. Define only expression_text(Tree, ParentPrecedence, Side, Text) in code/41_q2.pl for num(N) and bin(Op, Left, Right) trees. Check that 1+(2+3) and 1+2+3 remain distinguishable.
```
